Questions

Is there a shuttle to the Texas Medical Center?

Yes, and it is free. It runs Monday to Friday, 7:00 am to 7:00 pm, to anywhere within three miles, which covers the Texas Medical Center and MD Anderson. Ask the front desk to add you to the list the night before.

How far is the hotel from MD Anderson and the Texas Medical Center?

Under two miles. NRG Park is 2.6 miles, the Houston Zoo 1.9 miles, the Museum of Natural Science 2.4 miles, and Hobby Airport 7.5 miles.

Do the rooms have kitchens?

Every one of the eighteen room types has a kitchen with a full-size refrigerator, a cooktop, a microwave, dishes and a coffee maker.

Is there a medical rate for patients and caregivers?

Call the front desk on (832) 201-3131 and ask. MD Anderson advises patients to ask hotels near the Texas Medical Center for a medical or patient rate, and you may be asked for proof of patient status.

Are weekly rates available?

Yes, from seven nights. Click the "Book Now" button for the lowest available rate.

What about staying a month or longer?

Texas exempts stays of 30 or more consecutive days from hotel occupancy tax. Call the front desk before booking online so a long stay is set up correctly.

Is parking free?

Yes, on site and at no charge. There is truck parking as well.

Is there guest laundry?

Yes, on site, with washers, dryers and a folding table.

Are pets allowed?

No. Service animals are welcome at no charge.

Are there accessible rooms?

Five, both wheelchair and hearing accessible, with grab bars and roll-under sinks. Mention any other needs when you call and the front desk will note them.
